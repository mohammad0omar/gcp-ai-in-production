Section 4 of the Brightmoor Animal Rules requires every dog older than four months to hold a licence. The licence is renewed each year before 1 March. A licence costs 30 units, or 12 units for a dog that has been neutered. The tag must be worn on the collar in public. Guide dogs and assistance dogs are licensed free of charge. A dog without a tag may be held for up to five days before the owner is fined.
