Section 5 of the Brightmoor Waste Rules sets collection days. Household rubbish is collected on Tuesdays and recycling on Fridays. Bins must be put out by 07:00 and taken in by 20:00 the same day. Garden waste needs a paid permit sticker and is collected on the first Monday of each month. Bulky items such as sofas are collected only after booking, with a maximum of three items per visit.
