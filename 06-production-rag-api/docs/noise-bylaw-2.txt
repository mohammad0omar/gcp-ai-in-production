Section 2 of the Brightmoor Noise Bylaw sets quiet hours. Quiet hours run from 22:00 to 07:00 on weekdays and from 23:00 to 09:00 on weekends. During quiet hours, outdoor amplified sound must not be audible beyond the property line. Building work that makes noise is banned during quiet hours except for emergency repairs. A resident may report a breach to the city noise desk, which must answer within three working days.
