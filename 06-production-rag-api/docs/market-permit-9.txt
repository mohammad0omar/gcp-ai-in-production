Section 9 of the Eastfield Market Regulations says a stall holder needs a trading permit for each market day series. Permits are issued for 3 months at a time and cost 80 units. Food sellers must also show a valid hygiene certificate. A permit cannot be sold or lent to another person. A stall holder who is absent for three market days in a row loses the pitch, and it goes to the waiting list.
